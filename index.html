About

Pythonista for nearly 10 years, I’m an OpenSource Software Engineer at CourtBouillon 🍲.

We develop and maintain WeasyPrint and bring our expertise in automatic document generation to clients worldwide.

Still in the Python world, I organize meetups in Lyon (France), I have been part of the PyConFR organizing committee since 2015 and I was chair of AFPy (French-speaking Python Association) 🐍

Among that, I’m also a GDPR Consultant at Stella ✨
